--- title: On to Makuleke summary: Advanced Rifle Handling and Trails work up ahead tag: post date: 2026-11-02 ---

And just like that, we're done with the Tracks & Signs and Trailing courses.
I received a Level 1 competency for both, which was a massive relief.

No one on our course received more than a level one for Trailing, demonstrating either our collecive lack of competency or that learning to track an animal requires a lot more time and practice.

Makuleke awaits

In the far north of Kruger National Park, near the borders of Zimbabwe and Mozambique lies the Makuleke Concession. We'll be reunited with the Limpopo River, and have incredible opportunities for Astrophotography, Birding, and seeing a wildness that will put even Mashatu to shame.

We'll be there for our Advanced Rifle Course, which will involve a lot of drills with heavy rifles (.375 and .458) in the sweltering heat (40+ degrees).

I hope we can do some game drives and of course some birding, so I can fall in love with the bush again.
