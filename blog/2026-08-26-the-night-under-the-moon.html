--- title: The night under the moon summary: Sleeping under the hiding stars tag: post date: 2026-08-26 ---

Yesterday afternoon after lunch, we packed our overnight supplies and headed south to Pitsane Koppie

The koppie is located in one of the richer parts of the reserve, where we've had multiple sightings of mammals such as Lions, Leopards, and Elephants. The bird life too is incredible there. Great Egrets, storks, kingfishers and more.

The koppie itself is rather drab, grazed to the bone by the Wildebeest and Impala, with very few trees.

We arrived with an hour to spare before sunset, so we set about unpacking the vehicles, rolling out our mats and sleeping bags, and preparing a fire that would burn until sunrise.

Dinner round the fire was a combination of Stokbrot (or stickbread), Pap, and Seswaa. It was delicious.

The temperature began to drop as the moonlight took over as the primary source of light. Lions could be heard roaring in the distance, and Nightjars and Thick-Knees announced their intentions.
The stars were hiding though.

We were assigned nightwatch teams, and soon it was time for everyone to retreat to their sleeping bags.
My team's shift started at 2:30am, so we thought it prudent to get to sleep early. That plan fell by the wayside as my campmates talked and giggled till 10pm.

Sleeping in a sleeping bag has always been uncomfortable for me, tossing and turning as always, and finding sleep elusive. But somehow I fell asleep for a few minutes here and there, and was softly woken up by the previous nightwatch shift.

With my shoes back on, and headlamp attached, we resumed the 10-minute interval patrol. Nightjar's eyes reflected back from our spotlights, but thankfully there was nothing bigger nearby that could cause alarm.

Orion emerged from the East, and it didn't take long before our shift was over. As the moon set, some of the stars poked out, but it was nowhere near as vibrant as the full moonless nights we've experienced before.

After packing up, we headed back to camp, tired, but enriched.
