--- title: Tracking doldrums & Internships summary: Sweltering heat and the still seas of identifying tracks tag: post date: 2026-10-24 ---

I guess the romance period had to end at some point; I'm finding tracking to be the most obscure and unrewarding part of this whole course. And it's not just because I'm a novice; it feels so counter to what I want to be doing as a career in this industry. Before I get into that, there's something I need to cover first.

Internships

As part of our Professional Field Guide course, we're meant to spend 5-6 months at a proper lodge or project working as an apprentice guide.

So we've been sending out emails to our contacts and greater network, trying to find a suitable internship from this December to June 2027.

Most of the students are encountering difficulties such as a lack of responses, or their gender not being supported by the particular lodge due to accommodation scarcity.

The ideals

For my placement, and prospective career, I'd like to do something birding-related, whether it's as a guide, or in research or conservation. But in my position I can't be an idealist, and need to build experience before going down the route I yearn for.

I've got some amazing potential opportunities through my family friends, but working out the logistics for that is bottle-necked because of the three-party situation that is EcoTraining, the placement, and Me.

The conflict

Back to the problem at hand: when I think about tracking (and knowing tracks and signs, trailing an animal, etc), it only extends its usefulness as far as birding is concerned. I'm finding it less and less appealing to have to train to carry a rifle around in a dangerous game reserve, as the overlap for birding narrows.
And that feeds back into a demotivation for tracking as a whole.

Having paid for this one year course upfront means I'd be throwing away good money after bad, so it's a given I'll do the required courses anyway.

But the worst bit overall is that it's been swelteringly hot, I've had a consistent and nagging cough that won't go away, and a general lack of energy.

I'll keep trucking on, but wow, I wish things were clearer right now for all of us.
If you know anyone that needs an intern, please get in touch; our group of 11 students could do with a hand as we start our careers.
